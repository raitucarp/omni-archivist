Trillions of microscopic grains, untouched by fire, were gathering their aromatic mantles in the dark, laying the chemical foundations of worlds that would not ignite for another hundred million years. The *Sikhar* drifted among them, cold, silent, and whole—waiting patiently in the dark before the distillate clears.
