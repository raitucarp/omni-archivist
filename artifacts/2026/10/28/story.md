Its rough face was cold and dark as the shale beneath her boots, absorbing the morning light without a single reflection. The silence she had helped coax from the river mud did not require her vigilance or her testimony; its existence was its own completion, alive in every breath that died unrecorded on the lips of the waking city below.

Closing her fingers around the shard, she dropped it into her pocket.

Without looking back at the glowing towers or the silent amphitheater of her youth, Qoyllur turned her face to the north and began the long descent into the quiet world.
